Instant Pot White Chicken Chili

Creamy Instant Pot white chicken chili with shredded chicken, white beans, green chiles, and corn, finished with Monterey Jack, Greek yogurt, cilantro, and lime.

Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook ~25 min (+ pressure build/release) · Total ~45 min

Chili

  • 1.5–2 lb chicken breasts (thawed or frozen)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2½ cups chicken broth
  • 2 cans (15 oz) cannellini or great northern beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cans (4 oz) diced green chiles
  • 1½ cups frozen corn

To finish

  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt or sour cream
  • Chopped cilantro
  • 1 lime, juiced, plus wedges

Steps

  1. Set Instant Pot to Sauté. Heat oil, cook onion 3–4 minutes, then add garlic, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper for 30 seconds.
  2. Pour in broth and scrape the bottom clean. Add chicken breasts, beans, and green chiles. Cancel Sauté.
  3. Seal the lid. Cook on High Pressure 10 minutes for thawed chicken, or 15 minutes if frozen. Natural release 10 minutes, then quick-release the rest.
  4. Remove chicken and shred with two forks. Mash about a cup of beans against the pot to thicken. Stir chicken and corn back in.
  5. Optional: Sauté 2–3 minutes to thicken. Turn off heat, then stir in Monterey Jack and Greek yogurt until creamy. Add lime juice and adjust salt.
  6. Serve topped with cilantro, extra cheese, and lime wedges.

Notes

  • Use 2½ cups broth in the Instant Pot (less evaporation than stovetop). Always deglaze after Sauté to avoid a burn warning.
  • Even better the next day; keeps 4 days in the fridge or freezes well (add the yogurt when reheating).
  • For more heat, add a diced jalapeño with the onion.
