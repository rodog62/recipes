White Chicken Chili

Creamy stovetop chili with shredded chicken, white beans, green chiles, and corn, finished with Monterey Jack, Greek yogurt, cilantro, and lime.

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 40 min · Total 55 min

Chili

  • 1.5–2 lb chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cans (15 oz) cannellini or great northern beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cans (4 oz) diced green chiles
  • 1½ cups frozen corn

To finish

  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt or sour cream
  • Chopped cilantro
  • 1 lime, juiced, plus wedges

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium. Sauté onion 5 minutes, add garlic, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper and cook 1 minute.
  2. Add broth and whole chicken breasts. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered 15–20 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.
  3. Remove chicken and shred with two forks.
  4. Add beans, green chiles, and corn. Mash about a cup of beans against the pot to thicken. Return chicken and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Off heat, stir in Monterey Jack and Greek yogurt until creamy. Add lime juice and adjust salt.
  6. Serve topped with cilantro, extra cheese, and lime wedges.

Notes

  • Even better the next day; keeps 4 days in the fridge or freezes well (add the yogurt when reheating).
  • For more heat, add a diced jalapeño with the onion.
