Italian Turkey Stuffed Peppers

Bell peppers stuffed with lean ground turkey, spinach, rice, and marinara, topped with melted mozzarella. High-protein, about 45 minutes.

Serves 4 (6 peppers) · Prep 15 min · Cook 30 min · Total 45 min

Ingredients

  • 6 bell peppers
  • 2 lb 93% lean ground turkey
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 3 cups baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 1 cup cooked rice (about ⅓ cup dry)
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce, divided
  • ¾ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. If you don't have cooked rice, start ⅓ cup dry rice now.
  2. Cut the tops off the peppers and pull out the seeds and ribs. Stand them in a microwave-safe dish with a splash of water, cover, and microwave 4 minutes to soften.
  3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook the turkey and onion 8–10 minutes, breaking up the meat, until browned. Add garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper and cook 1 minute.
  4. Stir in the spinach until wilted, then the rice and 1½ cups of the marinara.
  5. Spread the remaining marinara in a 9x13 baking dish. Stand the peppers in the dish and pack them with the filling, mounding any extra around them.
  6. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes.
  7. Uncover, top with mozzarella and Parmesan, and bake 10 minutes more until the cheese melts.

Leftovers

Refrigerate up to 4 days. Reheat in the microwave 2–3 minutes, or covered at 350°F for 20 minutes. They also freeze well for up to 3 months.
