Cajun Salmon with Garlic-Spinach Orzo

Cajun-spiced baked salmon over creamy garlic-spinach orzo with Parmesan and lemon — about 25 minutes.

Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min · Total 25 min

Orzo

  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1½ cups orzo
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 5 oz baby spinach
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • Salt and pepper

Salmon

  • 1.5 lb salmon fillets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1½–2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • Pinch of salt (if your blend is salt-free)
  • ½ lemon
  • Green onions, sliced (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F and line a sheet pan.
  2. Melt butter in a deep skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds. Stir in orzo and toast 1 minute.
  3. Add chicken broth, bring to a simmer, cover, and cook about 10 minutes, stirring once or twice, until tender.
  4. Meanwhile, pat salmon dry, rub with olive oil and Cajun seasoning, and bake 10–12 minutes until it flakes (125–130°F for medium).
  5. Stir spinach into the orzo until wilted, then add Parmesan, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Taste first — Cajun blends are salty.
  6. Squeeze the remaining lemon over the salmon and serve over the orzo, topped with green onions.
