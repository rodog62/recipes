Sheet-Pan Teriyaki Chicken with Pineapple, Peppers, and Rice

Baked teriyaki chicken bites with pineapple, bell peppers, and red onion on one sheet pan, served over rice — about 40 minutes.

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 20 min · Total 40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken breast, cut in 1¼-inch cubes
  • ¾ cup teriyaki sauce, divided
  • 2 cups fresh pineapple chunks
  • 2 bell peppers, cut in 1¼-inch pieces
  • 1 red onion, cut in chunks
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1½ cups rice (dry), cooked per package
  • Green onions, sliced, for serving

Steps

  1. Toss chicken with ½ cup teriyaki sauce and marinate 15–30 minutes.
  2. Start the rice. Heat oven to 425°F and line a large sheet pan with foil (lightly oiled or sprayed).
  3. Spread chicken, pineapple, peppers, and onion in a single layer, not crowded — use two pans if needed so it roasts instead of steams. Drizzle veggies with olive oil and season lightly with salt and pepper.
  4. Bake 15–18 minutes, stirring halfway and brushing with the remaining teriyaki, until chicken reaches 165°F.
  5. Broil 1–2 minutes for some char, watching closely.
  6. Serve over rice topped with green onions and any pan juices or extra teriyaki.
