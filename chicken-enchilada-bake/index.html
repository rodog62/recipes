Chicken Enchilada Bake

Layered chicken enchilada casserole with black beans, corn, and cheese — high-protein, about 50 minutes (35 with rotisserie chicken).

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 35 min · Total 50 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb chicken breasts (or 4 cups shredded rotisserie chicken)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 can (19 oz) red enchilada sauce
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup frozen corn
  • 1 can (4 oz) diced green chiles (optional)
  • 8 small (6-inch) tortillas
  • 1½ cups shredded Mexican cheese
  • Plain Greek yogurt, for topping
  • Green onions or cilantro, for topping (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Rub chicken with cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Bake 20–22 minutes until 165°F, then shred. (Skip if using rotisserie chicken.)
  2. Lower oven to 375°F. Toss shredded chicken with black beans, corn, green chiles, and 1 cup of the enchilada sauce.
  3. Spread a thin layer of sauce in an 8x8 or 9x9 baking dish. Layer 4 tortillas (tear to fit), half the chicken mixture, and ½ cup cheese.
  4. Repeat the layers, then top with the remaining sauce and remaining cheese.
  5. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes. Uncover and bake 10 minutes more until bubbly.
  6. Rest 5 minutes before cutting. Top with Greek yogurt and green onions or cilantro.

Leftovers

Refrigerate up to 4 days. Reheat covered at 350°F for 15–20 minutes, or microwave single portions.
