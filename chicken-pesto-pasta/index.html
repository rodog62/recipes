Chicken Pesto Pasta

Seared sliced chicken breasts tossed with penne, jar pesto, cherry tomatoes, spinach, and sautéed onion, finished with Parmesan.

Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 25 min · Total 35 min

Chicken

  • 1.5–2 lb chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, and garlic powder

Pasta

  • 1 lb penne
  • 1 jar (6–8 oz) basil pesto
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 4–5 oz baby spinach
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced (optional)
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
  • Reserved pasta water

Steps

  1. Boil salted water and cook the penne until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain.
  2. Meanwhile, season chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Sear in olive oil over medium-high 5–7 minutes per side until 165°F. Rest 5 minutes, then slice.
  3. In the same skillet, sauté the onion 4–5 minutes until soft. Add cherry tomatoes and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Add the spinach and stir until just wilted.
  5. Toss penne with pesto, the vegetables, sliced chicken, and splashes of pasta water until glossy.
  6. Stir in Parmesan and serve with extra on top.

Notes

  • Thaw frozen chicken overnight in the fridge, or pound thin so it cooks evenly.
  • Leftovers keep 3–4 days; add a splash of water when reheating.
