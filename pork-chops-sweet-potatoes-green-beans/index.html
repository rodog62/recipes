Pork Chops with Sweet Potatoes and Green Beans

Skillet-seared pork chops with roasted sweet potato cubes and garlicky green beans — a simple weeknight dinner.

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 35 min · Total 50 min

Sweet potatoes

  • 2 lb sweet potatoes, cut in ¾-inch cubes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper

Pork chops

  • 4 bone-in or boneless pork chops (about 1 inch thick), thawed
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp butter

Green beans

  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss sweet potatoes with olive oil, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper; roast on a sheet pan 25–30 minutes, flipping once, until browned and tender.
  2. Pat chops dry and season with garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
  3. When the potatoes have about 15 minutes left, sear chops in a hot oiled skillet 4–5 minutes per side until 145°F. Add butter in the last minute and baste. Rest 5 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, steam or boil green beans 4–5 minutes until crisp-tender, drain, and toss in a pan with butter and garlic for 1 minute. Salt to taste.
  5. Serve chops with sweet potatoes and green beans.

Notes

  • Thaw frozen chops overnight in the fridge.
  • Thin chops cook faster — check temperature early to avoid drying them out.
