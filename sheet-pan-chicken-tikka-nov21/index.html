Sheet-Pan Chicken Tikka with Rice and Raita

Yogurt-marinated chicken tikka roasted on one pan, served over rice with a cool cucumber raita. High-protein, about 40 minutes.

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 25 min · Total 40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1½-inch pieces
  • 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt (marinade)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp chili powder (or ¼ tsp cayenne for more heat)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup dry basmati or long-grain rice
  • Raita: 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • Raita: ½ English cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • Raita: 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Raita: ¼ tsp ground cumin and a pinch of salt
  • Raita: 2 tbsp chopped cilantro (optional)

Steps

  1. Mix 1 cup yogurt, 1 tbsp lemon juice, garlic, garam masala, cumin, turmeric, ginger, smoked paprika, chili powder, and salt in a bowl. Add the chicken and stir to coat. Marinate at least 15 minutes (or up to overnight in the fridge).
  2. Heat oven to 450°F. Line a sheet pan with foil and brush it with the olive oil.
  3. Spread the chicken on the pan in a single layer, shaking off extra marinade. Roast 18–20 minutes until 165°F inside.
  4. Switch to broil and broil 2–3 minutes for charred edges. Watch it closely.
  5. While the chicken roasts, cook the rice according to the package.
  6. Make the raita: stir together 1 cup yogurt, cucumber, lemon juice, cumin, salt, and cilantro.
  7. Serve chicken over rice with raita on top or on the side.

Leftovers

Refrigerate chicken and rice up to 4 days; keep raita in its own container up to 3 days. Reheat chicken and rice in the microwave 2 minutes and add the raita cold.
