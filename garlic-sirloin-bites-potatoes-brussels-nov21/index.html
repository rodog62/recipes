Sheet-Pan Garlic Sirloin Bites with Baby Potatoes and Brussels Sprouts

Seared-style sirloin bites roasted on one pan with crispy baby potatoes and Brussels sprouts, finished with garlic butter. About 40 minutes.

Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 30 min · Total 40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb top sirloin steak, trimmed and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1¼ tsp salt, divided
  • ½ tsp black pepper, divided
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Use a large sheet pan, or two if yours is small, so nothing crowds.
  2. Toss potatoes and Brussels sprouts with 1 tbsp olive oil, garlic powder, ¾ tsp salt, and ¼ tsp pepper. Spread cut side down and roast 20 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, pat the steak dry and toss with ½ tbsp olive oil, smoked paprika, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp pepper, and the Worcestershire.
  4. Stir the vegetables and push them to the edges. Add the steak in a single layer. Roast 8–10 minutes for medium (135°F inside).
  5. Melt the butter with the garlic in the microwave, 30–45 seconds.
  6. Drizzle the garlic butter over the pan and toss everything together. Serve right away.

Leftovers

Refrigerate up to 3 days. Reheat in a hot skillet or at 375°F for 8–10 minutes so the steak doesn't overcook.
