Shredded Chicken Caesar Wraps

Lunch-prep wraps with shredded chicken in a lightened Greek yogurt Caesar dressing, romaine, and Parmesan. Makes 6, about 30 minutes.

Makes 6 wraps · Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min · Total 30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cup light Caesar dressing
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan (for the dressing)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 large head romaine (or 2 hearts), chopped
  • ¼ cup grated or shaved Parmesan (for the wraps)
  • 6 large high-protein or whole-wheat tortillas

Steps

  1. Put the chicken in a pot, cover with water by an inch, and add the salt and garlic powder. Bring to a boil, then lower to a gentle simmer and cook 15–18 minutes until 165°F inside.
  2. Let the chicken rest 5 minutes, then shred it with two forks.
  3. Whisk the yogurt, Caesar dressing, 2 tbsp Parmesan, lemon juice, and pepper in a large bowl.
  4. Add the shredded chicken and toss until coated.
  5. Lay out the tortillas. Fill each with romaine, about ¾ cup chicken, and a sprinkle of Parmesan.
  6. Fold in the sides, roll up tight, and wrap each one in foil or plastic wrap.

Leftovers

Assembled wraps keep 2 days in the fridge before the romaine softens. For the full week, store the dressed chicken (up to 4 days) and the romaine separately and roll each wrap the morning you eat it.
