Grilled Greek Chicken Rice Bowls

Lemon-garlic-oregano grilled chicken breasts over rice with cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, feta, and tzatziki.

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 20 min · Total 35 min

Chicken

  • 1.5–2 lb chicken breasts
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper

Bowls

  • Rice for 4
  • 1 cucumber, chopped
  • 1–2 pints cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ red onion, thinly sliced
  • Feta crumbles
  • Tzatziki (store-bought is fine)

Steps

  1. Start the rice.
  2. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Coat the chicken and let it rest 10–15 minutes while the grill heats to medium-high.
  3. Grill 5–7 minutes per side until the chicken reaches 165°F. Rest 5 minutes, then slice.
  4. Build bowls: rice, chicken, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, feta, and a big spoon of tzatziki. Extra lemon optional.

Notes

  • Oven option: roast at 425°F for 18–22 minutes if not grilling.
  • Leftovers keep about 3–4 days in the fridge.
