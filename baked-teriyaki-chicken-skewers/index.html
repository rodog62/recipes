Baked Teriyaki Chicken Skewers with Pineapple, Peppers, and Rice

Oven-baked teriyaki chicken skewers with pineapple, bell peppers, and red onion, served over rice — about 40 minutes.

Serves 4 · Prep 20 min · Cook 22 min · Total 45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken breast, cut in 1¼-inch cubes
  • ¾ cup teriyaki sauce, divided
  • 2 cups fresh pineapple chunks
  • 2 bell peppers, cut in 1¼-inch pieces
  • 1 red onion, cut in chunks
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Skewers (soak wooden ones 20 minutes)
  • 1½ cups rice (dry), cooked per package
  • Green onions, sliced, for serving

Steps

  1. Toss chicken with ½ cup teriyaki sauce and marinate 15–30 minutes. Soak wooden skewers in water.
  2. Start the rice. Heat oven to 425°F and line a sheet pan with foil; set a rack on top if you have one.
  3. Thread chicken, pineapple, peppers, and onion onto skewers. Brush veggies with olive oil and season lightly with salt and pepper.
  4. Bake 18–20 minutes, flipping halfway and brushing with the remaining teriyaki, until chicken reaches 165°F.
  5. Broil 1–2 minutes for some char, watching closely.
  6. Serve over rice topped with green onions and any extra teriyaki.

Notes

  • To grill instead: medium-high heat, 10–12 minutes, turning every few minutes and basting at the end.
