Sheet-Pan Lemon-Garlic Salmon with Asparagus and Rice

Salmon roasted with a lemon-garlic olive oil sauce alongside asparagus on one sheet pan, served over rice.

Serves 4Prep 10 minCook 15 minTotal 30 minDinner

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (about 1.5 lb)
  • 1 bunch asparagus
  • 2 lemons (1 zested and juiced, 1 sliced/for serving)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, plus a little for the asparagus
  • Salt and pepper
  • Pinch dried oregano (optional)
  • Rice for 4

Instructions

  1. Start the rice. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Mix the olive oil, garlic, lemon zest and juice, salt, pepper, and oregano.
  3. Snap the woody ends off the asparagus, toss with a little oil and salt on a sheet pan, and push to the edges.
  4. Pat the salmon dry, place skin-side down in the middle of the pan, spoon the garlic-lemon mixture over, and top with lemon slices.
  5. Roast 12–15 minutes, until the salmon flakes or reads 135–140°F.
  6. Squeeze fresh lemon over and serve over rice.
Leftovers: keep about 3 days. Reheat gently at 275°F.
